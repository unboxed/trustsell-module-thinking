The problem

Relationships don’t end with a bang.
They go quiet.

A seller’s world is huge — hundreds of contacts spread across inbox, calendar and Slack. Nobody decides to let one die. A champion just stops replying, a warm intro never gets used, a worry sits unanswered — and it all decays in the gaps between everything else.

The system around it

One brain. A team of experts. One connector.

Capability is distributed; intent is centralized.

People is one of the assistants — the people expert. It holds no goals of its own; it answers, and proactively reports what its brief tells it to watch.

The module

The expert on everyone you deal with.

It holds your whole world of people — and digs to find who matters.

Who they are
Name, role, company, where they sit — pulled together from every trace, not typed in by you.
Your history
Every thread, meeting and message with them, gathered into one picture — Tom, and your whole history with him.
Who knows whom
The web between people — champions, deciders, blockers, and the warm paths in.
The raw material

It starts from the traces you already leave.

Plain records, before any reading — the floor the reading stands on.

✉  Email
Messages, threads, contacts — who, when, what was said, what’s unanswered.
📅  Calendar
Meetings, who accepted or declined, recurring check-ins, free / busy.
💬  Slack
Channels, messages, mentions — the back-channel and who’s in it.
🌐  Web
Enrichment and trigger events — a new tender, a leadership change, funding.

It reaches these through the connector — it never holds the keys itself.

The heart of it

It doesn’t store relationships.
It reads them.

First it assembles — tidies scattered facts into one picture. Then it senses — reads meaning off that picture. The seam between counting and judging always stays visible.

The threshold

Is 26 days a lot? Only for this person.

There’s no fixed “21 days = quiet” line. The yardstick is the relationship’s own rhythm. If they reply within a day, 26 days screams. If they email twice a year, it’s nothing. Most of what looks like judgement is really deferred counting.

3 · The read

What it produces

Named reads, each carrying its evidence.

A signal is the unit — a reusable read that walks all the way back to a date in the inbox. They fall into three categories that all run at once.

Every signal earns its place by answering a real question a salesperson asks. The strategy just tilts where the emphasis goes.

The rules it plays by

It suggests. You decide.

You work the verbs
Decide, approve, send. The AI works the nouns — find the target, draft the message, gather the proof.
Nothing goes out alone
Every read is a suggestion. A human pulls the trigger — at least for now.
Gaps named, not guessed
When the ground isn’t there — a source unconnected, a fact only you know — it says what’s missing instead of inventing.
How might we

Make someone’s whole world of relationships visible — so they always know who to nurture, grow, and plant?

That’s what we’re here to imagine — what this module looks like, living, inside the app.

Three lenses to ideate through
