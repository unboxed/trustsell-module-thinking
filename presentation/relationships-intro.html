The module

People: the expert on everyone you deal with.

Three things it knows about each person.

Who they are
Name, role, company. Pulled together from your emails and calendar, not typed in by you.
Your history
Every message, call and meeting with them, gathered into one picture.
Who knows whom
How people connect: who can introduce you, and who you still need to reach.

People is one part of Trustsell. It works out who matters from what people actually do.

What it can pull together

It gathers scattered things into one view.

The kinds of things it can put in front of you.

Person history
Everything about one person in one place: emails, calls, meetings.
Conversation history
A whole thread or chat, in order, with everyone on it.
Meeting history
A call gathered up: who was there, and the notes if they were taken.
Stakeholder map
Everyone at a company, by role, including who you are missing.
Deal
One selling effort: the people and conversations in it, across companies.
What it can spot

It points out things worth your attention.

Grouped three ways: keep relationships alive, move them forward, find new ones.

Sustain
Keep relationships alive.
Gone quiet
Someone you usually hear from has gone silent.
Loose end
Something you said you'd do, still not done.
Champion went quiet
A strong contact you can't reach lately.
Advance
Move things forward.
Champion
Someone inside the company pushing for you.
Blocker
Someone slowing things down or pushing back.
Not landing yet
They keep asking the same things; it hasn't clicked.
Expand
Find new ones.
Warm way in
A person you know who can introduce you.
New person
A new name worth knowing has shown up.
Buying interest
Signs a conversation is getting serious.
How you can trust it

It shows its reasons, and leaves the call to you.

Shows its reasons
Every result comes with the facts behind it, so you can check.
You decide
It suggests, you choose what to do. Nothing is sent for you.
Tells you what's missing
If it can't be sure, it says so instead of guessing.
Looks like Tom has gone quiet
No reply in 3 weeks
He usually replies within a day
You normally talk every month
Facts you can check yourself.
The challenge · How might we

Make someone's whole world of relationships visible, so they always know who to sustain, advance, and expand?

Sustain = keep relationships alive. Advance = move them forward. Expand = find new ones.
