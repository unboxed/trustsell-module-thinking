Crazy 8s

Fold a page into eight. One sketch per panel — eight ideas in eight minutes.

Hands-on · 02

Landing page for your idea

Prompt your idea to Claude or ChatGPT as if it were a real product — let it generate the landing page.

You're prompting the intent, not the visuals — what it is, who it's for, why it matters. The model builds the page.

10 min
