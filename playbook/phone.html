Why isn't this relevant?

The phone is where you answer. The desk is where you act.

Ask cards are built for a phone. "Ten seconds. Best while you remember" is a card you answer walking out of the building, and early on the home is mostly Ask. So the phone carries the learning curve.

Act cards come to the phone when the noun is a short note you would happily send from Mail. A brief, a demo plan or anything you would want to edit stays at the desk, and the card says so.

  • Swipe left is Not Now. The card leaves with its one line.
  • Swipe right opens the noun in a sheet. It never sends. Mail does, when you do.
  • On an Ask card a swipe right does nothing. I don't guess an answer from a gesture.
  • Not Relevant stays a button. It opens the reasons, and the one-tap why is where I learn.
  • The thinking is the next page. Scroll down and the card snaps to it; scroll back up for the card.

Drag with the mouse or a finger, or use the buttons. Five of today's twenty-two cards fit a phone: the ten-second Asks and one short note. The rest wait for the desk.
