# The documents

This world is the patient one again: almost every document is yours, filed in Drive, and a card
that names one in `documents:` is saying what to send. The buyer's own documents matter too, but
they are published rather than handed over, so they live on the open web and not here.

| Document | id | What it is | Dated |
|---|---|---|---|
| The business case | business-case | What a change would be worth at one organisation, built from their own published numbers. The thing you reach out with, not a brochure | per account |
| The two-minute video | demo-video | Priya's recording of the part of the system somebody asked to see. Sent so a "just show me it" does not wait three weeks for a diary | per question |
| The rail case study | rail-case-study | What a comparable group did and what it saved them, written up with their name on it | 2025, and it shows |
| The security answers | security-answers | Your standing answers to the questions an IT department always asks, out of the bid repository | kept current |
| The lost-time note | lost-time-note | One page on what other groups did to halve lost-time injuries, and how long it took them | 2026 |
| The consultation note | consultation-note | One page on what other groups did about works council consultation, and how long it took. The step your buying process says you can partly help with | January 2026 |
