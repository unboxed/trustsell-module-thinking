# Make it easy for Yvonne Pryce to make the introduction she promised

## What happened

- Why now: nine weeks is long enough that she has almost certainly forgotten, and short enough that reminding her is still a favour rather than a complaint.
- Her words on 16 July, in her mail and on the call the day before: "I'll get you in front of Alan, he runs the digitisation plan". That is a thing she meant, not a thing she committed to by a date, so I grade it down. It is still the only route to IT anybody has named.
- Stanmore's own annual report, published in April, puts the asset register at the centre of a five-year digitisation programme. That is the plan Alan runs, in their words, and it is what the note opens on rather than on her promise.
- Alan Beck is on no thread and in no diary. Nobody new from Stanmore has appeared since July.
- An IT discussion is the middle step of a buy here, and the one that decides how long everything after it takes. It is not a nice-to-have that has slipped.
- She is your champion and she is busy. The move is not a reminder, it is giving her something Alan would actually want, so the introduction costs her one line.
