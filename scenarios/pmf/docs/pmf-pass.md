# The broker's world, worked from both ends

Job 3 of `NEXT.md`, run on the world the library was not derived from: a merchant cash advance
broker, twelve cards, a tempo of hours, measured against the volume seller's twenty-two questions
(`V`). The same six steps as the council world, per person, and the record grows a section at a
time as each group of people is worked. Up from the records in `world/records.md`, through the
counts, to what fires; down from the seller's questions; the cards opened last. The question held
throughout is the one `NEXT.md` sets: when a read that fires at the patient end cannot apply here,
does the question it answered there have any other answer in this world?

## The top of the ladder: Tony Prieto, Paula Larkin, Alma Vega, Renee Sackville

Worked on 22 September 2026, with Marcus Bell and Hannah Beck beside them. Four cards, one per
person: the offers Tony has not heard, the cost Paula is owed in writing, the winter Alma has
told you about, and the statement Renee has not sent. These are the four cards that turn on hours,
and the first test of the message rows at the fast end.

### 1. The records

Written in `world/records.md` as fiction, keeping every fact the cards implied where the facts
could all be true, and settling where they could not.

- **The clock.** Every row carries a time. The tool's day begins at 08:00 on Monday 21
  September, and the last of Tony's four answers landed at 08:20. "Since Friday" on the cast's
  line and "Monday" on the card's timeline could not both be true of all four answers; the records
  put Northline's decline and Avondale's two offers on Friday, the last two after you had stopped
  for the day, and Bayard's this morning. The card says so now.
- **Text is the conversation.** Each merchant has one form, one text conversation of a handful
  of messages, and an upload. The only mail with a merchant is Marcus's thread with Paula, because
  the offer went by mail. Nothing is by phone that the tool can read, and three calls that matter
  (yours with Paula on the 7th, Marcus's with her on the 17th, and whatever was said to Tony) are
  on no record.
- **Alma sent a year, not three months.** The card said her statements "cover enough periods to
  show the swing", and three summer statements cannot show a winter. She sent twelve months from
  her banking app because it was easier than picking three, which is the kind of thing that
  happens, and the twelve are in the section month by month.
- **Paula's cost is half in writing.** Marcus's mail of Thursday names the amount and the term
  and not the total repayable or the daily debit; his call may have said both. That is the fact
  the card turns on, and it is written down rather than assumed either way.
- **Renee's file went short on Thursday evening**, in Marcus's own note on the CRM, and nobody
  has told her. The request named June, July and August, so the one still out can be named.
- **The processor is on record only where he writes.** Marcus's activity lines and his one mail
  thread are what the tool holds of him; his calls are not. Hannah Beck is a funder's person, and
  nothing counts a funder as a person: her answers are counted against the deals they land on.

Five things the counts said about themselves while being run:

- **`required-disclosures` at zero silences the read, and the card had not noticed.** The read's
  own rule is that with no told rule "there is nothing to check against and the read is silent
  rather than lenient". The card said the rules were unwritten in its *Still unclear* and rested on
  the read anyway. Both cannot hold. A told record nobody has filled produces an Ask, which is the
  twenty-second pass's lesson arriving at the fast end.
- **`since-an-answer-landed` counts working time and the card needs hours.** The count says "the
  working time between the day the newest answer was recorded and now"; on a morning where the
  newest answer is forty minutes old, the honest figure is the clock, and the section gives it.
  The count's words say "day"; the arithmetic does not care.
- **Three statements cannot show a season.** `money-in-swing-vs-own-periods` says its own floor is
  about six periods, and the trade asks for three. A read about seasonal income at this end
  depends on a merchant sending more than was asked for, or on a bank connection the trade wishes
  for and nobody offers. The card stands on a year because the fiction gave it one.
- **A pattern the count sees and cannot name.** $612 to "Ford Credit" on the 3rd of every month
  is `repeated-outgoings-to-one-payee`'s whole job, and without the told names in the field it is
  a payee and nothing more. That is `ask-known-lenders`' case, made on a real row.
- **A call leaves nothing, three times in four people.** What was said to Paula on two calls
  decides whether the cost was disclosed; what was said to Tony decides whether he has heard. The
  reads say so, and at this tempo it is most of what happens.

### 5. Where the two ends meet

Opened last. Four verdicts from `NEXT.md`.

**`act-tony-offers`**

- answer-came-back: **both ends reach it**, and the seller's end has no question for it, which
  the read already says. The timeline was wrong about when three of the four landed and says so
  now. The message rows: opens on the promise it keeps, points at what came back and what is still
  out, asks nothing and gives a time, holds back the terms. At this tempo the gift and the next
  step share one text, which the *asks* row says outright.

**`act-paula-disclosure`, which is `ask-disclosure-rules` now**

- disclosure-still-owed: **both ends reach the question, and the read is silent.** V18 is live
  today, on a merchant agreeing, and the read cannot fire because nothing is told. The Act was
  right about what a careful broker would do and wrong about what the tool could know. It is an
  Ask now, on the live case: what must be in writing before a merchant agrees, with Paula's
  numbers drafted and waiting on the answer. Told once, the Act follows as a card the answer turns
  up. *(My reason, not yet yours.)*

**`act-alma-winter`**

- uneven-income and can-they-carry-it: **both ends reach it through the wrong order.** The card
  rested on the carrying read, which cannot be summed before an offer and says only a shape; what
  fires high is the income read, on her own year. Swapped: the swing is the read, the carrying is
  why it matters. The rows open on her own dates, point at her own figures, ask one fact, and hold
  back any daily amount that is not on an offer.

**`act-renee-statements`**

- missing-documents, V14: **both ends reach it**, the plainest card in the world. Its message had
  no sanctioned opening: nothing owed, nothing published, no angle, and the note opens on the two
  statements that came. What they sent, by name, is an eighth kind of opening now, in `build.js`
  and `message-shape.md`: the one thing you both already know, and a chase that opens anywhere
  else reads as a form letter. *(My reason, not yet yours.)*

**Supply with no demand**: `where-this-one-goes` fires on Vega and says Avondale misses on
industry, and no card carries it because Marcus had already submitted. Argued right for today; the
read's moment was Thursday, before the file went out, and nothing in the library says a read
should arrive before the step it would have changed. `buying-intent` fires on all four and carries
nothing, because at this end a yes is the first rung and not a finding.

**Demand with no supply**: V7, because the read that answers it assumes several people and the
fact that answers it here, the owner's signature on the form, is read by nothing as an answer. V10,
because the form's own words about urgency are on the timeline and no read takes them. V18 until a
rule is told. And the three calls, which is a data gap the trade lives in.

### 6. Decided

- `act-tony-offers`, `act-alma-winter` and `act-renee-statements` are `decided` and carry their
  message rows. `act-paula-disclosure` is retired and `ask-disclosure-rules` stands in its place,
  `decided`.
- `answer-came-back`, `missing-documents` and `uneven-income` carry `assumes_status: decided`.
- `documents-received` is a sanctioned opening (`build.js`, `docs/message-shape.md`), and the
  table of what each writing read contributes has three rows from the fast end.

Findings for the library, recorded in `coverage.md` and not fixed here:

- At the fast end the read that answers "who decides" cannot be made, and the fact that answers
  it, the owner's signature on the application, is read by nothing as an answer to V7.
- The form's own words about urgency ("this month if possible", "not desperate") are on the
  timeline and no read takes them; V10 stays answered in part.
- `since-an-answer-landed` speaks in days and the tempo is hours; the arithmetic is fine and the
  words are not.
- A season cannot be read off the three statements the trade asks for; `uneven-income` at this end
  depends on the merchant sending more than was asked, or on a bank connection nobody offers.
- Nothing says a read should arrive before the step it would have changed: `where-this-one-goes`
  fired on Vega after the file had gone out.
