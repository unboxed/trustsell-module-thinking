# The cast

Everyone a card can be about. `you` is the seller the tool is speaking to.

Two halves, and they are the other way round from the patient sale. There, one seller faced several
people at each buyer. Here there is **one owner per business** and **two people on your own side**:
you until the offers are in, your processor after that. Nobody is sure where the line falls, which
is the scenario's own open question and not a gap in the library.
