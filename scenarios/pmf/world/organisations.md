## The funders

Where the money actually comes from. A funder does not stand on the ladder: the ladder is where a
deal stands, and a deal stands with one funder at a time or with several at once.

Each has a box: the industries it will take, the credit band it will go down to, and the size of
advance it wants. Which funder a deal should be submitted to is exactly the read the library does
not make yet, and the reason this scenario was written (V15 in
[`docs/coverage.md`](../../../library/docs/coverage.md)).

| Organisation | id | What they take |
|---|---|---|
| Avondale Capital | avondale-capital | Restaurants and retail, weaker credit, $10,000 to $60,000. Answers the same day |
| Kestrel Funding | kestrel-funding | Any industry, good credit only, $75,000 upwards. Slower, and the cheapest rate on the shelf |
| Northline Advance | northline-advance | Trucking, freight and plant. Wants two years in business |
| Bayard Funding | bayard-funding | Small and fast. Under $25,000, almost anyone, and the dearest money you can place |
