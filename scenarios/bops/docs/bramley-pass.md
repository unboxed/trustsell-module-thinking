Three things the counts said about themselves while being run:

- **The roles list cannot say "split".** `people-covered-vs-needed` holds the map against the
  told roles, and the told role is "a head of service with the budget", one person. At Bramley
  that is two people, one reached and one not, and the count has to report the row as covered or
  missing when it is both. Felton's pass found a role off the list (finance); Bramley's finds a
  role on the list that is two roles here. Both are the same fact: the profile's cast is a
  template, and a council's is not.
- **A buyer's document on a mail attachment is gathered and counted by nothing.** `document-set`
  takes `gmail#attachment`, so Jonathan's spreadsheet is on the shelf; `documents-received` and
  `documents-still-out` need `handover#document-request` and `handover#handed-over-document` only,
  so in a world without the handover the read above them is silent and the one document a buyer
  has sent is invisible to it. The same goes for the figures inside it: `figures-on-record` stands
  on `document-set`, and every count over it needs the handover too. Named, not fixed, because no
  card in this world would quote the count: nothing at Bramley is still out.
- **A title without a signature has to come from somewhere, and the count keeps where.**
  `head-of-signature-scan` reads signatures, Contacts and the web, and says which. Ravi's title
  is from none of those: it is in a colleague's introduction, which the count reads as a message
  and can hold. The fiction had it in the wrong column; the count was right.

## 5. Where the two ends meet

Opened last. Four verdicts from `NEXT.md`, on the one card.

**`act-karen-budget-holder`**, re-tested from the budget holder's end

- missing-people, Q4, Q6 and F2: **both ends reach it**, and the card was already right about
  the move and the order: after the note, not beside it; a fact, not a name. Martin Hodgson is
  among its `about` now, so the figure rule reads his section, and its Sources row says where

- **The card holds his name back, and the count agrees.** `decision-maker-reached` says its

  `people-covered-vs-needed`. That was the right choice before anyone knew why.

  no change to its reads.
