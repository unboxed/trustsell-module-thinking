The fifth person run through the method in `NEXT.md`, Job 1, on 22 September 2026: Lisa Holmes,
one person with two cards, both resting on `time-to-reconnect`, chosen because her world has
what Neil Chapman's did not, a real published reason. Up from the records in `world/records.md`,
through the counts, to what fires; down from the seller's questions; the two cards opened last.

## 1. The records

Written in `world/records.md` as fiction, keeping every fact the two cards implied where the
facts could all be true, and settling the places where they could not.

- **Seven messages since April, last on 20 August.** Held as written: three threads, all yours,
  three replies from her, each within three working days.
- **"She rarely writes first."** The records say never. Three threads is under the line for a
  pattern, so the cards say the fact and not the pattern.
- **"Published ten days ago."** The strategy went out on Friday 11 September; from the world's
  today that is five days and three working days. The card had counted from somewhere else.
- **"The sector is still talking about it."** Nothing on record. Removed.
- **What she said in August.** The card said only "last on 20 August". The records give her
  words: "nearly there, we publish in September, I'll be in touch after", because a congratulation
  that lands three working days after she published is a different note from one that lands
  after she has broken a promise, and the read that watches her promise needed the sentence.
- **No use-cases told.** The world has a goal and a target shape and not one use-case typed for
  the offering, and nothing before this pass said so. The told table now does, because the match
  between page 12 and East Marston depends on it.

Two things the counts said about themselves while being run:

- **`time-to-reconnect` failed on Lisa the way it did not fail on Neil.** Neil had the quiet and

Findings for the library, recorded in `coverage.md` and not fixed here:

- `time-to-reconnect` is on no card in the shipped world after this pass. Two people were joined
  to it for a reason to write; the read is for a relationship that drifted, and the reason to
  write on a live contact is `stated-priorities` (theirs) or `lead-with-this` (yours).
- No Ask asks the seller for the offering's use-cases, and `lead-with-this` is silent on your
  side without them. `ask-good-fit` asks for the target shape, which is a different told record.
- `their-promise-undelivered` holds a loose promise ("I'll be in touch after") without grading
  it, as its `breaks` line says; a card that writes to her the week she published has to know it
  is there so as not to read as a reminder. The message rows carry that in "holds back".
- **A card on a published read cannot reach the mail on its own.** `stated-priorities` reads
  the web and nothing else, so a card resting on it alone may not quote a thread. Both cards
  needed a second read to reach Gmail, and the build said so before anything was committed. The
  right second read was there (they-owe-you); where there is none, the card would have to drop
  what it says about the correspondence, which is the rule working.
