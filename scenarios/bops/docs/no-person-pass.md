The last four provisional cards in this world, run through the method in `NEXT.md`, Job 1, on 22
September 2026: the four Asks that are about you and nobody else. Who you know at the twelve
councils with no name (`ask-twelve-councils`), what a good fit looks like (`ask-good-fit`),
whether to drop the greetings (`ask-openings`), and what a buy at a council takes on their side
(`ask-buying-process`). Every one of them rests on something the seller has not told the tool

one agreed on Karen Hughes (`karen-hughes-pass.md`). With these four, every card in `bops` is
decided and every card is figure-checked.
