## 6. Decided

- The three cards' `signal_status`, `supporting_status` and `counts_status` are `decided`.
- `cooling-champion` lists `who-starts-threads`, for its second half. *(My reason, not yet
  yours.)* `assumes_status` on cooling-champion, loose-end and missing-people is `decided`: each
  was tested here against the word it claims and needed it.
- **Proposed, not built:** a card may not state a figure the world does not carry, checked by
  `build.js` the way Sources rows are. On this one person it would have failed three figures on one
  card. It needs a records file per person to check against, and only Karen has one, so building
  it now would fail twenty-five cards for a reason that is not theirs.

Findings for the library, recorded in `coverage.md` and not fixed here:

- `reply-gap-vs-own-rhythm` says "median gap between their replies" where the card wanted the
  reply delay. Both are real; the count's words should say which it is, and the number widget
  should say which it shows.
- `who-starts-threads` summarises over three months. Its rows carry the fact; its summary loses it.
- `innocent-explanation-scan` names calendar records that are not visible for a buyer.
- `promise-made-undelivered` has no rule for a promise re-made.
- `days-open-vs-own-rhythm` measures a thread's pace, and short threads have none. The person's
  pace was what the card needed, and it is in a different count.
- `cooling-champion` never checks the person was a champion. Champion does, and is now supporting.
