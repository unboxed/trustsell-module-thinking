# North Ridley, worked from both ends

The ninth person run through the method in `NEXT.md`, Job 1, on 22 September 2026: Michelle
Turner, one person with three cards, two of which arrive the day before a demo and one the day of
it. Up from the records in `world/records.md`, through the counts, to what fires; down from the
seller's questions; the three cards opened last. The method is the one agreed on Karen Hughes
(`karen-hughes-pass.md`). This is the first run of the understanding reads from the records up,
the first world with a room full of people who have never written, and the first card whose day
is fixed by an event in the calendar rather than by a read.

facts could all be true, and settling the places where they could not.

  that exists.
- **What North Ridley published.** Nothing in the world said. The records give the council a plan,
  page 9, 4 March 2026: "give our frontline officers time back". That is the world absorbing what
  `stated-priorities` needs; without it the officer-time angle rests on the thread alone, and no
  count under an opening read counts what a thread keeps asking about (below).
- **The invite.** Michelle's, in person, committee room 1, no Meet link. Eight invitees besides
  you, none on an event with you before, five on nothing before. Five accepted, three have not

Four things the counts said about themselves while being run:

  *(My reason, not yet yours.)*

  resolves a role from a signature, a contact, a title on the web. Michelle's message of 1

- **What a thread keeps asking about is counted only under `pushing`.** Five of fourteen messages
  ask about officer time, and the count that holds it, `things-they-asked-you-for`, is the
  envelope: it says what a draft may give, under a read that fires when a draft would push. No
  opening read has a count of the thread's subject, so the brief's angle rests on what they
  published, and the thread is corroboration written in plain words.
- **How your own demos went is read by nothing.** The brief holds compliance back because the one
  recorded demo's compliance section drew no question. `talk-time-on-calls` needs three captured
  calls and reads the buyer's share, not what a slide drew. The claim rests on the transcript
