# Moreton, worked from both ends

The tenth and eleventh people run through the method in `NEXT.md`, Job 1, on 22 September 2026:
Julie Barker, who has left, and Sanjay Mistry, whom her mailbox named. One card, arriving the
morning her automatic reply does. Up from the records in `world/records.md`, through the counts,
to what fires; down from the seller's questions; the card opened last. The method is the one
agreed on Karen Hughes (`karen-hughes-pass.md`). This is the first run of `champion-went-dark`
from the records up, the first card written to somebody with no record at all, and the first
time a card's person and its records belong to two different people.

## 1. The records

Written in `world/records.md` as fiction, keeping every fact the card implied where the facts
could all be true, and settling the places where they could not.

- **A bounce and an auto-reply cannot both come from one address.** The card's source row said
  "the bounce this morning, 08:12, and Julie's auto-reply", and the cast said her email bounced.
  A bounce means the server refused the message; an automatic reply means it was delivered and
  the mailbox answered. The two counts under the read keep them apart on purpose, and the card
  was leaning on both. Settled as the automatic reply: your note of Thursday 17 September at
  08:10 was delivered, and at 08:12 her mailbox answered, "I have left Moreton Borough Council.
  For BOPS matters please contact Sanjay Mistry, sanjay.mistry@moreton.gov.uk. This mailbox is
  not monitored." `bounce-back-detected` is 0 and came off the card. The cast, the design log and
  the story's News example now say auto-reply rather than bounce.
- **"This morning" is Thursday.** The card arrives on 17 September and says "this morning"; the
  cast, written for a today of 16 September, said the same. The records put the reply on Thursday
  morning, the day the card arrives, and the cast row says Thursday.
- **"9 messages since April", "she started threads", "asked for pricing in May".** Held as
  written and given dates: four threads from 13 April, two opened by her; nine messages to 30
  June, five hers; one call, on 28 April, at her suggestion; and on 12 May, "roughly what would
  it cost a borough our size? And is there any way to start smaller than the whole thing?",
  answered the next day with the first band and a yes, in prose, because the partial rollout
  sheet did not exist until July.
- **What she left behind.** The records add what the card did not say and the read needs: on 30
  June she wrote "the programme board has been pushed to September. I'll come back once it has
  met." That is why you wrote on 17 September, and it is a promise of hers that left with her.
- **Sanjay.** Nothing between you: no message, no event, no call, not in Contacts. Two records
  hold him, both in Julie's section: the automatic reply, and the council's digital programme
  page, checked the same morning, which lists him as Digital Programme Manager and does not list
  Julie. He has a section of his own so that the figure rule reads his cards against something,
  and it says the records are hers.

Four things the counts said about themselves while being run:

- **A buyer's promise does not die with the buyer.** `their-promise-undelivered` keeps "I'll
  come back once it has met" open past the automatic reply that says she has gone, because
  nothing tells it a promise is void when the person who made it leaves. On this world it is
  harmless, because `they-owe-you` does not fire on a two-turn thread with no usual; on a longer
  one it would chase a successor for a predecessor's word. The message row that holds her promise
  back is the card's own doing, not a count's.
- **The name a departure notice hands on reaches no placing count.** `auto-reply-no-longer-with`
  keeps the name, and its own body says a successor named is a door. But the counts that place
  a new person, `prior-history-scan` and `head-of-signature-scan`, sit under `new-stakeholder`,
  which fires only when the name appears on a thread or an event. A name inside the text of an
  automatic reply is on neither. The card's "appears nowhere in your mail" and "Digital Programme
  Manager" are those two counts' work, and the card reaches them through gmail and the web
  directly, as North Ridley's brief reached Michelle's message naming who was coming.
- **The origin gather cannot see this door.** `route-in-on-record` reads a lead entry, a form, a
  told source or the person's own history; an automatic reply on a colleague's thread is none of
  them. So `warm-path`, whose first count this is, has no bridge to weigh, and the one person who
  could have introduced you has left. F17, how did I come by this person, has its answer on record
  and no count that reads it.
- **A month in their words is not a date.** "Pushed to September" is the only timing Julie ever
  gave. `dated-facts-in-their-words` keeps it and `time-to-their-date` has nothing to count down
  to. It is why you wrote on the 17th, and it was a reason in their words that no read could
  quote.

## 5. Where the two ends meet

Opened last. Four verdicts from `NEXT.md`.

**`act-sanjay-mistry`**

- champion-went-dark, Q18 and Q6: **both ends reach it.** The read fires on a fact, and it answers
  the question the seller asks first about a note that came back from a mailbox. The card is right
  about the move.
- missing-people: **reached through no read the card named.** "Your only door there just changed
  hands" is `rests-on-one-person` at 9 of 9, and "I'll look for a second door" is the read's
  question, who else. Now supporting, and the card says nobody else at Moreton has ever been on a
  thread.
- buying-intent: **both ends reach it, softly.** The card quotes Julie's price question, and that
  read is where the count lives. It fired in May, not today, and it is why the note to Sanjay can
  say what Julie had. Kept as supporting, for that reason and no other.
- bounce-back-detected: **a figure the world could not produce.** There was no bounce. Came off.
- The message rows: **the opening had no sanctioned ground.** The table in `message-shape.md`
  says a note after this read opens on the name the auto-reply gave, and the build's list of
  openings did not include the count that holds it. Added: `auto-reply-no-longer-with` is a
  sixth kind of opening, the name a departure notice handed you. *(My reason, not yet yours.)*
  The rows then hold: it points at what Julie had, asks nothing, and holds back her figure and
  her promise, because neither is his.
- The card's people: the figure rule reads `to` and `about`, and every figure on this card is
  Julie's. She is now among the card's `about`, so the card is checked against her records.

**Supply with no demand**: none. Every read that fires is carried by the card.

**Demand with no supply**: Q8 and F17, on Sanjay. Whether anybody can introduce you is asked and
nothing counts it, because the origin gather does not read a colleague's automatic reply and
LinkedIn is held back. The card answers F17 in the draft's first line by hand.

## 6. Decided

- The card's `signal_status`, `supporting_status` and `counts_status` are `decided`, and it
  carries its message rows.
- `champion-went-dark` carries `assumes_status: decided`: it fired because `was-once-active`
  held on nine messages and a call; without that history the automatic reply would have been
  news about a stranger. *(My reason, not yet yours.)*
- `auto-reply-no-longer-with` is a sanctioned opening for a message (`build.js`,
  `docs/message-shape.md`).
- The bounce is an automatic reply, in the cast, the design log and the story's example.

Findings for the library, recorded in `coverage.md` and not fixed here:

- A buyer's promise does not die with the buyer: `their-promise-undelivered` keeps it open past
  the notice that says they have left.
- The name a departure notice hands on reaches no placing count: the two that place a new person
  sit under `new-stakeholder`, which needs the name on a thread or an event.
- The origin gather cannot see a door opened by an automatic reply, so `warm-path` has nothing
  to weigh and F17 is answered by hand.
- A month in their words is kept by `dated-facts-in-their-words` and counted down to by nothing.
