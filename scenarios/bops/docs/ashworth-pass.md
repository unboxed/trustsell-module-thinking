colleague in partnerships, who says he knows her. Two cards: let Ashworth rest, arriving on 16

Four things the counts said about themselves while being run:

- **Two counts under one read disagree about what a link is.** `mutual-contact-count` reads
  Slack messages, so Simon's line counts as a bridge. `intro-chain-length` reads Contacts and
  LinkedIn, so from what it needs there is no path at all. Both sit under `warm-path`, and on
  this world the read has a bridge with a chain length its own count cannot see. The card quotes
  the one and not the other.
- **A colleague's offline contact is on no told record.** The offline-touch record is yours. What
  Simon did at the LGA event reaches the tool only because he happened to say so in the
  workspace, and `firm-contact` says as much: a colleague's own contact is invisible unless it
  reaches a shared place. The event is undated, so `since-last-colleague-touch` counts the
  mention and not the contact.
- **The scan has two blind spots on a person outside your organisation.** Her calendar cannot
  be read and LinkedIn is held back, so leave and a job move, the two commonest innocent
  explanations, would both look like silence. `enough-tries` says its scan is empty and the card
  says where it could not look.
- **Four notes went with nothing the tool could have opened on.** No use-case told, nothing
  published by Ashworth, no door on record. Had the tool been asked to draft any of the four,
  `message-shape.md` says it would have refused: there was no gift, no reason and no angle. The
  notes were written before the tool, and the record of them is what lets `enough-tries` say

## 5. Where the two ends meet

Opened last. Four verdicts from `NEXT.md`.

**`act-ashworth-rest`**

- enough-tries, Q19 and V5: **both ends reach it.** The read whose whole job is to say stop
  fires, and the seller's question is whether to chase again. The card is right about the move.
- one-sided: **both ends reach it through the wrong join.** The card said "you are doing all the
  work", and that is true, but the read that says it needs a history to read balance across and
  Nicola has never written a word. The card was never about balance; it was about stopping.
  Came off, with its counts.
- champion-went-dark: **kept for its counts.** `bounce-back-detected` and
  `auto-reply-no-longer-with` are 0, and the card quoted them for "no bounce, no auto-reply".
  That fact is `innocent-explanation-scan`'s, under the read that fires. Came off.
- warm-path: **the other card's read.** The rest card names Simon and quotes him, and that fact
  is `colleague-touches-on-record`, which sits under `enough-tries` since 21 September: a person
  who has had four approaches has had four whichever desk they came from. Came off.
- **The last note had nothing to open on.** The card carried a draft, "One More Try", a fifth
  note that said you had written before and asked for a name. Every sanctioned opening in
  `message-shape.md` is something of theirs or something you owe, and there is neither: the note
  opened on the silence itself, which is exactly the message `pushing`'s second shape describes
  and `enough-tries` says not to send. The card's own copy agreed ("a fifth message is unlikely
