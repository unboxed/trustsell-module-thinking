# Westmoor, worked from both ends

The second and third people run through the method in `NEXT.md`, Job 1, on 22 September 2026:
Gary Pearson and Rachel Gill, who share one thread. Up from the records in `world/records.md`,
through the counts, to what fires; down from the seller's questions; the four cards opened last.
The method is the one agreed on Karen Hughes (`karen-hughes-pass.md`); this is its first run on
two people at once, and the first where the world was written for a person who was not the card's
subject (Priya Nair, whom Gary brought to the demo).

## 1. The records

Written in `world/records.md` as fiction, keeping every fact the four cards implied where the
facts could all be true, and settling the places where they could not.

- **Thirty-one messages.** `act-gary-records` said "your mail with Gary since February: 31
  messages". Nothing decided that number; it was written with the card. The records hold
  thirty-two messages on eight threads, because that is what the threads the cards imply add up
  to once each has a beginning and an end.
- **Rachel's second question was on a Saturday.** `act-rachel-peer-call` dated her messages 9 and
  12 September. 12 September 2026 is a Saturday. The records put it on Friday 11 September, two
  working days after the first.
- **"Twice in four days", "eight days", "six days".** All three counted on a calendar. The library
  counts working days and Karen's records already said so. Eight days since the promise is six
  working days; six days since Gary's question is four; twice in four days is Wednesday and
  Friday.
- **"He is building his coalition for you."** The card said it with one colleague in evidence,
  Rachel. `champion` needs colleagues brought in, plural, so the records give Gary one more:
  Priya Nair, the service manager he brought to the demo in March, now in `cast.md`. That is the
  world absorbing what the card claimed rather than the card claiming what the world lacks.
- **Who set up the intro call.** The Ask said only "your calendar". The records make Gary the
  organiser, because his message of 26 August says "I'll set something up" and a champion
  arranging the meeting is the sign the read looks for.
- **The director.** Gary mentions his director three times and never names her. The records
  keep her unnamed and off every thread, because `organisations.md` says "no budget holder seen
  yet" and a name would have made that false.

Three things the counts said about themselves while being run:

- **A question the buyer asked you had no count.** `ask-made-unanswered` counts questions
  **you** put to **them**. Gary's card quoted it for his question to you, and so did a card in the
  enterprise world for a buyer's question about price. Both cards were right about the world and
  wrong about the arithmetic under them, and the day's order was resting on the wrong count,
  because it is on the list of what is owed. `their-question-unanswered` now exists, under
  `loose-end`, which has a third shape; `ask-made-unanswered` came off the owed list, because a
  question of yours they have not answered is them owing you.
- **`who-starts-threads` again.** As with Karen, its three-month summary window holds 1 of 2 and
  says nothing, while the whole history says 5 of 8. Twice now the fact a card wants has been in
  the rows and not in the summary.
- **`objection-entry-exists` cannot tell "no entry for this worry" from "no entries at all".** In
  this world nobody has typed a single worry or answer, so every worry on record is a gap. The
  read over-reports, which its own body admits, but the first thing it should say in that case is
  that the seller has never been asked. Nothing asks: `ask-good-fit` asks about fit, not worries.

## 6. Decided

- The four cards' `signal_status`, `supporting_status` and `counts_status` are `decided`.
- `their-question-unanswered` exists, under `loose-end`, and is on the owed list in `build.js`;
  `ask-made-unanswered` is off it. The enterprise world's price card moved to the new count too.
  *(My reason, not yet yours.)*
- **Built:** a card may not state a figure the world does not carry. `build.js` checks every
  card whose people have a section in `world/records.md`, against that section, the organisations,
  the documents and the goal; each written section ends with a "What the counts give" table so the
  figures the counts compute are on record beside the count. Seven cards checked today, eighteen
  skipped. A planted figure fails the build.

Findings for the library, recorded in `coverage.md` and not fixed here:

- A customer's standing offer to be a reference is on no count.
- `objection-entry-exists` cannot say "nobody has told me any worries", and nothing asks.
- `who-starts-threads` loses the fact in its summary window, for the second person running.
- `internal-advocacy-mentions` was being read off future intent ("I'll put it to procurement");
  on the records three real claims exist, so the card was right by accident.
- A card's `arrives` is the world's fiction, not the read's: a read can fire nine days before its
  card is shown, and nothing says which is right.
- Nothing checks the claims a draft makes.
