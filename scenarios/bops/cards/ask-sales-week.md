# How much of your week goes on selling?

Last week I brought you twenty cards, on Wednesday, Thursday and Friday. Whether that is a day's work or a fortnight's depends on how much of your week is selling, and you have never said.

## Known so far

- Twenty cards last week: ten on Wednesday, six on Thursday, four on Friday. I spread them as if you sold every working day.
- You told me at setup that you sell alone, with a colleague in partnerships beside you. Nothing says how much of your week that takes.
- Fifteen notes have gone since 17 August. That is what you sent, not when you sit down to it.

## What's missing

- How much of your week is selling. It is on no record I can read, and it decides how many cards a day I should bring and how hard to press.

## What your answer changes

- Most days: the week stays spread across the days, one move per person at a time.
- Two or three afternoons: I hold what can wait until you next sit down, and bring only what would be gone by then.
- When I can: each time you open, the one thing that matters most comes first.

## Where your answer is saved

- In your profile, as your time and cadence, with the date. I keep it honest against when you actually come back.
