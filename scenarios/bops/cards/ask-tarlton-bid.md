Clarification questions close on Friday and the tender closes on 9 October. Whether to bid is yours: nothing I hold says what a bid would cost you against what it could win. Your answer sets what I do with Tarlton for the next fortnight.

## What's missing

- Not sure: I ask again on 5 October, with whatever the portal has said by then.

## Where your answer is saved
