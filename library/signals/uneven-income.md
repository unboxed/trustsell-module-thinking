# Uneven income

## What it means

What this buyer takes in does not arrive evenly. Some periods are far above their own usual and
some far below, on the figures they have shown you.

It is a read of shape, not of size. A small steady income and a large uneven one are different
answers to different questions; this one says only whether the months look alike. It describes,
and does not judge the sale: [`can-they-carry-it`](can-they-carry-it.md) is where an uneven income
meets a fixed repayment.

## In practice

A lead has sent six months of statements. Money in runs close to one figure for four of them and
drops to under half of it for two, which sit together. The read: "on the six months you have, what
comes in is uneven: two months well under their own usual, side by side. Six months cannot say
whether that is a season. Ask for the year if it matters."

## How it is worked out

Applies the shared reading rules in [`docs/reading-principles.md`](../docs/reading-principles.md),
and the rules for what is never read into a document in
[`channels/handover.md`](../channels/handover.md).

- Trigger: money in per period (counting) is set against the buyer's own median across the periods
  on record (counting), the own-rhythm rule applied to money. The read lands when some periods sit
  far from that median. The one judgement is how far counts as uneven, and it is never a fixed
  share of income. Where that line sits is not written yet, and the read says so rather than
  picking a number.
- Confidence: high when many periods are on record without a gap and every figure was read from a
  document they handed over; softens when the periods are few, when a gap breaks the run, or when
  the only figure is one they said rather than showed. On fewer than twelve months it can say
  uneven and never seasonal, and says which.
- Needs: what the buyer hands over. Without it the read has only what they said on your form or
  the thread, one figure with no periods to compare, so it cannot be made (a data gap, named not
  faked). Cash that never reaches a statement is invisible, and a figure said on an uncaptured
  call is a told gap until you say so.
- Assumes: nothing about the shape of the sale. It stands on the buyer's own documents alone, so it
  holds on a lead of a fortnight as well as on a customer of years. It needs a run of periods, but
  that is what the documents carry, not a history with the person.
- This read points neither way. It says what the figures show; whether that matters for this sale
  is the read above it, and what you do is yours.
