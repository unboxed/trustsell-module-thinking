# What their side still has to do

## What it means

A buy needs work inside the buyer's organisation, and some of it shows no sign on the record.

You know what a buy at this kind of buyer takes, because you have done it before: the case somebody
has to write, the assessment their rules require, the committee that has to sit. The read holds
your list against this deal and says which steps nothing has been seen of, and which of those you
said you could help with.

It also says what the pattern looks like. A buyer whose steps are being ticked off without you is
running a process; a buyer who has started none of them and is deep in conversation with you is
still working out what they want. Those are different sales and they are worked differently.

## In practice

A buy at this kind of buyer takes four steps on their side: a case for the spend, a data
assessment, a committee date, and a signature. Two months in, a date has been named for the
committee and nothing has come across about the other three. You said you can supply the figures a
case needs and a template for the assessment. The read: "three of their four steps have shown no
sign. You can help with two of them and have not offered. The one that has moved, the committee
date, they set without you."

## How it is worked out

Applies the shared reading rules in [`docs/reading-principles.md`](../docs/reading-principles.md).
Borrows Offerings' told fit-shape to know which buyer's process applies.

- Trigger: the told process for this buyer's shape has at least one step, and at least one of those
  steps has no sign on the deal's record (counting).
- What it says: which steps have no sign, which of those you offered to help with, and the shape of
  the pattern. The last of those is the F6 half: steps moving without you is a process being run,
  no steps moving while the talking goes on is a buyer still deciding what they want. Both are read
  off the same arithmetic.
- Confidence: never high, and this is the one read in the library where that is a property of the
  question rather than of the evidence. Work done inside a buyer's organisation is mostly invisible
  from outside: a case can be written, circulated and approved without a line of it reaching you.
  So the read grades on how visible a step is by nature. A committee date somebody has to tell you
  is close to a fact; a case nobody would ever send you is close to a guess, and the read says
  which kind each one is instead of averaging them into one number.
- **It always suggests asking, never concluding.** The honest output is "I have seen no sign of
  these three, is that right", and the answer is a told fact the tool keeps. A read that announced
  a buyer had not done their homework, off records that could never show it, would be wrong often
  and insulting when it was. *(My reason, not yet yours.)*
- Needs: no source beyond the deal and your own list. The list is a told thing: where you have told
  it no process for this shape, there is nothing to hold the deal against and the read is silent (a
  told gap, named not faked). It does not supply a buying process of its own, because a generic one
  would be indistinguishable to you from the one you described.
- Assumes: a conversation already under way, because a step shows a sign on a record that exists.
  Before there is a deal there is nothing to hold the list against (an assumption gap, named not
  faked).
- Keep it apart from [`missing-people`](missing-people.md), which is the same shape of question
  about **who**: the people a decision needs whom nobody has reached. This is the same about
  **what**. The third seller says no system does this one, which is why it is worth having.
