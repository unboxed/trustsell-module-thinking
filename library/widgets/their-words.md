# What they said

## What it shows

The exact words that make the card true, quoted rather than retold. A repeated worry, an
objection, a line that names someone new. It lets you judge the words for yourself instead of
taking the tool's reading of them.

Quote only what was written or recorded. A paraphrase is not their words, and belongs in the
story.

## Row form

`<who> · <when> · <the words>`

The words go in without quotation marks; the widget draws them.
