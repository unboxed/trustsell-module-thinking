# A draft

## What it shows

What the sheet does when the tool has written something for you: a message, a brief. You change
it by asking in the last row, and the tool rewrites it in place.

On the cards today a draft is raised by an action (`opens: draft`) rather than named as the
reply. Whether it becomes a `reply.module` of its own is not decided.

## Row form

No rows. The words come from the card's `## The draft` section.
