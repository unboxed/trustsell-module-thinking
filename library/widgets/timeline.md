# Timeline

## What it shows

The moments that explain the card, in the order they happened. A message history is a timeline,
and so is a deal's run of meetings. Today sits on the line, so you can see how long ago
everything was, and a date still to come (a deadline) can sit after it.

Keep it short. Four or five moments carry a story; twenty are a log.

## Row form

`<when> · <what happened>`

The first part is a date as the card would say it ("20 August", "March") or "Today". The
second is one short sentence. The row marked "Today" is the filled dot.
