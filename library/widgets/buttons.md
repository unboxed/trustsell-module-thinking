# Buttons

## What it shows

The reply for something ready, or something to confirm. The signal writes the words on the
buttons (That's Right and Not Quite; Send the Note and Not Relevant; Got It). Most cards end
here.

## Row form

No rows. The words come from the card's `actions`.
