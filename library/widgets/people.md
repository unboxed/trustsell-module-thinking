# People

## What it shows

Who is involved: the people on a deal, or in a room. Each has a role and, where the tool can
say it, where they stand. It is the widget for "who is for it, who is against it, and who is
missing".

A person the tool knows only by role ("one from Finance") is a row too. That gap is part of
the picture.

## Row form

`<name> · <role> · <where they stand>`

The third part may be empty when nothing is known.
