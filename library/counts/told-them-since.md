# Anything sent since it landed
## What it counts

Messages from you to the buyer on this deal's conversations, sent on or after the day the answer was
recorded. Zero is the number that matters, and it is the whole of the second half of the read above
it. Which records it needs and when the number stops meaning anything are not written in the count's
fields yet, like every other count.

It counts within [`conversation-history`](../assemblies/conversation-history.md), which since 21
September gathers text as well as mail, decided when the count was written rather than derived.

## What it cannot see

A call. This is the count most likely to be wrong in the seller's favour, because passing news on by
voice is the natural way to do it and leaves nothing to count. It also cannot see a message sent by
a colleague working the same deal unless the firm shares its records, and it cannot see a channel the
seller has not connected: on both, a zero means "nothing I can see" and the read says so.
