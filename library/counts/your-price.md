# Your price
## What it counts

Your own price for one offering, exactly as you told it: the figure or the band, how much discount
room is yours, the term it runs for, and how it is paid. No arithmetic beyond reading the fields
and saying which are empty.

It is the plainest count in the library and three reads need it for entirely different reasons: to
compare, to weigh what a buyer can bear, and to put in front of somebody before they agree.

## What it cannot see

Anything negotiated on this deal, which is `answers-back`'s. The shelf price is what the product
costs; what this buyer was quoted is a different fact and the reads are told to prefer the
second.
