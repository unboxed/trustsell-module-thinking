# Things they asked you for
## What it counts

Everything the buyer has asked you for across this deal: a document, a number, an introduction, a
demonstration, an answer. One row per request, with the words, the day, and whether it has been
met.

It is the supply of gifts. Every message the tool drafts has to name the gift, and this is the list
the gift comes from, which is why it is counted separately from an open ask: the same fact serves
as a debt to one read and as an opening to another.

## What it cannot see

Anything asked for on an uncaptured call, and anything they wanted and did not ask for.
