# Evidence on file
## What it counts

Everything on the evidence shelf, one row per document, with what it is meant to prove, when it was
made, who it is about and what kind of proof it is.

It is the shelf itself, tallied, and it is the floor under every read that asks whether there is
anything to send.

## What it cannot see

Evidence nobody filed, and evidence filed without a tag saying what it proves, which the shelf
cannot use even though it holds it.
