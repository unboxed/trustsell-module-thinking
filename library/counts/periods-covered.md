# How much of a year the documents cover
## What it counts

How many periods the documents on record cover, and whether they run without a gap. A season needs twelve months to show; six months can show uneven, never seasonal, and the read says which it can say.

It counts within [`figures-on-record`](../assemblies/figures-on-record.md), what the buyer's documents show. Which records it needs and when the number stops meaning anything are not written in the count's fields yet, like every other count.

## What it cannot see

Cash that never reaches a statement, and a period no document covers. A figure the buyer said rather than showed is kept apart by the gather and is not summed here.
