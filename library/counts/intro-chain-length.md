# Steps to an introduction
## What it counts

The shortest route from you to the target through people you deal with: one step (you deal with
somebody who knows them), two steps, or further. One row per route, shortest first.

One step is an introduction. Two is a favour. More than two is not a path, and the count reports
the length rather than pretending distance does not matter.

## What it cannot see

Whether anybody on the chain would actually make the introduction, which is the whole question and
is on no record anywhere.
