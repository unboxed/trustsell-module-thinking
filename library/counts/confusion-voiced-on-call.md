# Confusion said on calls
## What it counts

Each passage on a captured call where somebody shows they have the wrong picture: a question with a
wrong premise, a restatement that misses, a term used to mean something else. One row per passage,
with who said it.

The same four things the written counts look for, heard rather than read, and kept as the passage
so it can be checked.

## What it cannot see

Every uncaptured call. Also the confusion of the person who says nothing, which on a call is easier
to have and harder to see than in writing.
