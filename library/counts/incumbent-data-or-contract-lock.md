# What locks them in: data or contract
## What it counts

For each lock-in mentioned, which kind it is: the contract (a term or a notice period), the data (a
migration, an integration, years of records), or the habit (training, process, a team that knows
it). One row per mention, sorted, with the words.

They matter differently. A contract has a date and will end. Data and habit have no date and are
the ones that keep a buyer where they are for a decade.

## What it cannot see

Which kind actually binds them, as against which kind they mention. People quote the contract
because it is concrete and stay because of the habit.
