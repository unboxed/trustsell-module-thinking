# Where a claim comes from
## What it counts

For each claim, who is behind it: your own marketing, a named customer, an independent third party,
or nothing identifiable. One row per claim.

It is the first thing a sceptical buyer asks and the thing sellers are least used to being asked,
which is why it sits under a read whose job is to stop a message making a claim it cannot stand
behind.

## What it cannot see

A third-party report your own firm commissioned, which is independent in form and not in fact. The
count records who published and the read is told to discount accordingly.
