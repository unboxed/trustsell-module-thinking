# Fits what they said matters to them
## What it counts

For one buyer organisation, each priority they have stated in their own published words, and which
of your told use-cases speaks to it. One row per priority, with the use-case where there is one and
nothing where there is not.

It counts within what **they** published rather than within your own catalogue, which is the
correction of 21 September: a count labelled "fits what they said matters to them" that counted
over your own material could never have meant anything.

## What it cannot see

A priority they hold and have not published, which at a private company is nearly all of them. And
the difference between a priority and a page of values.
