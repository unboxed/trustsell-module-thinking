# Good-fit signs present
## What it counts

For one lead, each factor of the told fit-shape it meets, with the evidence and where it came from.
One row per factor met.

Three columns rather than two, always: met, missed, and unknown. Folding unknown into missed is the
commonest way a fit score lies, because it turns "we have not looked" into "they are wrong for
us".

## What it cannot see

Anything true of the organisation and not published or said. Larger organisations publish more, so
this number systematically favours them, and the read is told as much.
