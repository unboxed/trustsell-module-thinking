# Steps the buy takes
## What it counts

The steps a buy at this kind of buyer takes on their side, counted from what you told it, in the order you put them. Where you have told it nothing for this shape, the number is zero and that is a told gap rather than a buy with no steps. Which records it needs and when the number stops meaning anything are not written in the count's fields yet, like every other count.

It counts within [`buying-process`](../assemblies/buying-process.md).

## What it cannot see

A step you know about and never wrote down. The count is exactly as good as the list, and the list is yours.
