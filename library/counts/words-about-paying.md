# What they have said about paying
## What it counts

Each time, since the deal completed, that the customer has raised paying on one of the deal's conversations: asking to pause, to move a date, to reduce an amount, or saying one did not go through. Who said it, when, and in which words. A plain list, most recent first. It counts what was said, never what it means.

It counts within [`since-the-sale`](../assemblies/since-the-sale.md), the deal since it completed. Which records it needs and when the number stops meaning anything are not written in the count's fields yet, like every other count.

## What it cannot see

Anything said on a call nobody recorded, or to a colleague rather than to you, which is a told gap until someone says so. And the difference between ordinary housekeeping and real difficulty, which is a judgement one floor up and often not there either.
