# Good-fit signs missing
## What it counts

For one lead, each factor of the told fit-shape it demonstrably fails, with the evidence. One row
per factor missed.

Missed needs evidence exactly as met does. The asymmetry that creeps into every fit score in every
tool is that a match needs proof and a mismatch does not, and holding both to the same bar is the
whole of this count's job.

## What it cannot see

The same publication bias as its sibling, in the other direction: a small organisation that
publishes nothing will look neither good nor bad, which is the honest answer.
