# Offerings in play
## What it counts

How many offerings are on the shelf, how many of those the goal is pinned to, and how many of those have a told fit-shape complete enough to match a lead against. Three numbers, not one, because they fall away in that order: a shelf of six can be four in play and two with anything to match on.

Where the first number is one, there is nothing to choose between, and the read above says so instead of comparing.

It counts within [`catalogue`](../assemblies/catalogue.md), everything you sell. Which records it needs and when the number stops meaning anything are not written in the count's fields yet, like every other count.

## What it cannot see

A thing you sell and have never told the tool about, which is simply not on the shelf. A supplier you could place a buyer with but have not set up as an entry. And whether two entries are really the same thing from two suppliers or two different things, which is a judgement nobody has asked it to make.
