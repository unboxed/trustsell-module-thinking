# What they have said or shown they can bear
## What it counts

Per period, money in less what already goes out, from the figures the buyer's documents state. Where
the buyer has shown nothing and only said a figure (on your form, on the thread), that figure
stands in, kept apart as said rather than shown so the read can grade it lower. No share of income
is applied here: the room to leave under the line is the read's judgement, not this count's.

It counts within [`figures-on-record`](../assemblies/figures-on-record.md), what the buyer's
documents show. Until 21 September it counted within `document-set` and said "not written yet".
Which records it needs and when the number stops meaning anything are not written in the count's
fields yet, like every other count.

## What it cannot see

A period no document covers, cash that never reaches a statement, and what goes out to anyone the
documents do not name. Over six months it cannot see a season.
