# Top worry for this kind of buyer
## What it counts

For a kind of buyer, the worries on record across deals with that kind, ranked, with how often each
appeared and the gap between the first and the second.

The gap is what makes it usable. One worry on eight of ten deals is worth pre-empting in a first
message; three worries on four each is not, and leading with any of them is a guess dressed as
preparation.

## What it cannot see

Buyers of this kind you never reached, whose worry you therefore never heard. The list is built
from conversations that happened.
