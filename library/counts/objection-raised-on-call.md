# Worries raised on calls
## What it counts

Each worry raised on a captured call, one row per passage, with who said it and the day. It feeds
the same pile as the worries raised in writing, so an objection made in a meeting is an objection.

The passage travels with it, because a worry summarised is a worry changed.

## What it cannot see

Every uncaptured call, and every worry raised in a meeting you were not in, which is where a deal
is usually lost.
