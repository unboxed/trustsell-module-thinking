# Reply speed, against usual
## What it counts

The median working time between your message and their reply over the last month, against the same
median across the whole history, with the direction of travel. Speeding up and slowing down are
both readings; the count supplies the two numbers and the gap between them.

Speed is the earliest of the warmth signs and the least reliable on its own, which is why the reads
that use it always pair it with something else.

## What it cannot see

A fast reply written by somebody else on their behalf, and a fast reply that says nothing. It also
cannot see the reason: a week of speed before a deadline of theirs is not the same as a week of
speed because they like you.
