# Kind of evidence an answer needs
## What it counts

For each worry, the kind of evidence that would answer it: a certificate, a reference, a
measurement, a demonstration. From your told proof tags.

It is the sibling of `grounding-kind-required`: that one is about a claim you make, this one is
about a worry they raise.

## What it cannot see

A buyer who would accept something else entirely, and one who would accept nothing.
