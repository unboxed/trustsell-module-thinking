# Steps that moved without you
## What it counts

Of the steps with a sign, the ones whose sign shows the buyer did it on their own: a date they set, a document produced internally, a name that appeared without an introduction. Against the ones that moved in a conversation with you. Which records it needs and when the number stops meaning anything are not written in the count's fields yet, like every other count.

It counts within [`buying-process`](../assemblies/buying-process.md).

## What it cannot see

The reason. A step run without you may be a buyer with a process, or a buyer who has stopped involving you, and the two look identical from outside.
