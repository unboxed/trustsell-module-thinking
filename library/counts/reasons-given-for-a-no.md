# Reasons given for a no
## What it counts

Every reason on record against the ended deals in a set, counted by how often the same reason comes back, in the words it was given in. A reason is only counted where one was actually recorded, whether you typed it or the deal record carried it. Deals that ended without a reason are counted as a number of their own, not shared out among the reasons that were given.

Grouping two differently worded reasons as the same reason is the one place this count stops being arithmetic. Where two wordings plainly mean the same thing (the price, the cost, too expensive) they are counted together and the wordings kept; where it is a judgement, they stay apart and the read above decides.

It counts within [`deal-outcomes`](../assemblies/deal-outcomes.md), the pile of ended deals. Which records it needs and when the number stops meaning anything are not written in the count's fields yet, like every other count.

## What it cannot see

The real reason, as against the stated one. A buyer who says the price and means the risk is counted as the price, and no read above may correct them: a stated reason is a fact and the reason behind it is not on record anywhere. Reasons nobody wrote down, which is most of them when the CRM is not connected. And a reason given to a colleague rather than to you.
