# Your effort, against theirs
## What it counts

A tally on each side of the things that actually passed between you: documents sent, names given,
questions answered, meetings accepted, introductions made. One row per thing, with its day and
direction, and a total each way.

It is the second number behind "one-sided", and the more honest of the two: a thread can be short
on both sides and perfectly even, or long on both sides and entirely one way.

## What it cannot see

Anything given off the record: a favour, a word in the right ear, a piece of advice on a call. The
count is a floor under a read about balance, and the read is told plainly that the ledger it sees
is the written part of a relationship, not the whole of it.
