# Who writes first
## What it counts

Of all the threads between you and this person, how many each of you started, as a count and as a
ratio, and the same split over the last three months against the whole history. Both halves
matter: a relationship where you have always opened every thread is a different thing from one
where they used to write first and stopped.

A thread is started by whoever sent its first message, not by whoever the subject is about.

## What it cannot see

A conversation that began on a call or at an event and only reached mail afterwards, which reads as
you starting it. And a person who replies to everything within the hour but never opens anything,
who may be attentive rather than passive; that is the read's call.
