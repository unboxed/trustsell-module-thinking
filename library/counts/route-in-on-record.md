# How you came by them, on record
## What it counts

Whether any record carries the door this person came through, and if so which one and on what day:
a list a colleague handed over, a form they filled in, a told line of yours. Plus the permission
that comes with it, whether you may say so out loud, which is carried from the record and never
inferred. Which records it needs and when the number stops meaning anything are not written in the
count's fields yet, like every other count.

Two signals quote it. [`warm-path`](../signals/warm-path.md) reads it for the introduction door.
[`lead-with-this`](../signals/lead-with-this.md) reads it for the line a first message opens on.

It counts within [`where-it-came-from`](../assemblies/where-it-came-from.md), decided when the
count was written rather than derived.

## What it cannot see

A door nobody wrote down, which is most of them until a seller starts telling the tool. It counts
"no record" as no record, never as a cold approach: a person you met at a conference two years ago
and never logged looks exactly like a name you found in a search, and the difference is yours to
supply.
