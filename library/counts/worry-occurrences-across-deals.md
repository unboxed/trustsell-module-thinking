# Same worry across deals
## What it counts

For each distinct worry, how many deals it has appeared on, how many times in total, and over what
period, with the trend. One row per worry.

A worry on nine of your last ten deals is not an objection, it is a gap in what you sell or how you
describe it, and this is the number that turns a hundred conversations into that sentence.

## What it cannot see

A worry that stopped appearing because you stopped selling to the people who have it.
