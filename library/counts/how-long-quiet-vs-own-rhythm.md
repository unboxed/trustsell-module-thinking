# Their silence, against usual
## What it counts

How long since anything passed in either direction, in working days, against the median gap across
your whole history with them. Both numbers, never the ratio alone.

It differs from `reply-gap-vs-own-rhythm` in what it measures: that one is a reply outstanding,
this one is a relationship gone quiet, and a relationship can be quiet with nothing owed by
anybody.

## What it cannot see

Contact off the connected channels, which makes a live relationship look dead. And a person whose
median is built from a single intense fortnight two years ago.
