# How close you were
## What it counts

A picture of the relationship at its strongest: how many turns passed, over how long, how many
meetings, and how even the giving was. One set of numbers for the whole history, and the period
they belong to.

It is what tells a reconnection from a cold approach. Writing to somebody you worked with for a
year is a different act from writing to somebody who answered you twice in 2021, and the message
that treats them the same is the one that lands worst.

## What it cannot see

Everything that made it close and left no record: the drink after the conference, the favour, the
thing you sorted out for them. A warm relationship conducted mostly by phone reads here as a thin
one.
