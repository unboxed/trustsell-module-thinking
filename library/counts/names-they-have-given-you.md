# Names they have given you
## What it counts

Each person the customer has named to you, on the deal's conversations or since, who is not already in the deal: who they are, how the name arrived (mentioned, copied in on a thread, handed over as a contact), how many times, and whether they are at the customer's own organisation or another one. The two are counted apart, because a name in their own organisation is a new person in the same account and not an introduction outward.

It counts within [`since-the-sale`](../assemblies/since-the-sale.md), the deal since it completed. Which records it needs and when the number stops meaning anything are not written in the count's fields yet, like every other count.

## What it cannot see

Anything the customer handed over for another purpose. A name on a bank statement or an invoice is out of reach by rule 3 of *What is never read*, whatever it would be worth. Without LinkedIn, whom the customer actually knows is invisible, so only the names that reached your own threads are counted.
