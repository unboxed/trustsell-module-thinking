# What competitors charge
## What it counts

The published prices of the named rivals in this field, as a band with each rival's figure and its
source page and date. Where a rival publishes nothing, that is a row saying so, not an absence.

A band rather than an average, because an average of three published prices and two holes is a
number that looks solid and is not.

## What it cannot see

Everything negotiated. Published prices are the list, and in most markets the list is where a
conversation starts. It also cannot see what a rival actually charged a buyer like this one.
