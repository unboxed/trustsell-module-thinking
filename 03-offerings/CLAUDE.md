# 03 · Offerings

**Role:** Reason to *answer*. Holds (or fetches) everything about what we sell: what each offering is, who it fits, pricing, positioning, objection-handling, and its natural sales motion (land-and-expand vs one-and-done). **Knowledge, not goals** — "push this product" is a *priority* set by `04` / spine, not a property here.

**Answers questions like**
- Which offering fits [this customer profile], and what's the entry use-case + price?
- For [their specific objection], what's the rebuttal and the proof?
- What's the natural next step in this product's motion?
- Any pricing / packaging lever for a cost stall?

**Open questions**
- Where does "how this product is sold" (motion) end and spine tactics begin?
- Structure: per-offering files? which fields?
