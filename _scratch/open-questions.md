# Open Questions — Parking Lot

Running list of unresolved decisions. We did a full module-by-module pass and settled the big structural questions (each now recorded in its module's `module.md` and libraries). What remains here is mostly the **how** — deliberately deferred until we work through concrete use cases.

## Resolved (now living in the module docs)
- **Standing strategy** → it's **per-offering goals owned by the spine**; `04` holds no strategy.
- **The learning loop** → **distributed**; the spine keeps only outcome attribution and routes learnings back to the owning module.
- **Module boundaries** → `02` = the outside world + all relationship history + sensing; `03` = product knowledge; `04` = the organisation (optional; a *source* of materials + a *fence* of compliance/brand); `05` = voice only.
- **`01`** → a port/switchboard with no agency; modules reach *through* it. It is not the sensor or the trigger source.
- **Autonomy** → **people as the fulcrum, AI as the lever**: the AI suggests, the human decides.
- **Sensing** → a **three-floor** build, not two: Connections does a **deterministic** fetch of raw facts (floor 1); `02` does the still-deterministic **counting** over them (floor 2 — the *checkable* floor); then `02` does the **non-deterministic** reading into actionable signals (floor 3). The opinion-line sits *inside* `02`, between the counting and the reading — not at the Connections handoff. Grounded in the *connected* accounts (Gmail, Calendar, Slack, web/Tavily); a signal is only surfaced if it can become an action. Recorded in `01`/`02`, traced in `docs/tracing-back.md`.
- **The trigger model** → **both**: a default beat plus events. A routine's shape (trigger · inputs · job · push-condition · output) is recorded in `00`, worked out in full in `02`.
- **Raw material** — two piles: **fetched** (mail, calendar, Slack, web, through Connections) and **told** (the goal, the seed list of who to chase, your voice and preferences). *Characterisation is not told* — a contact's disposition (champion, cooling, blocker) is **inferred from behaviour** by `02`, never tagged by the user. Recorded in `02`; worked in `docs/scenario-councils.md`.
- **The unit of a target** — a **deal**: the selling effort People gathers (its offering, its conversations, and the people on them, which can span more than one organisation), placed by the Brain on the goal's ladder. The coalition (deciders, influencers, champions, blockers, and the gaps) is the deal's *people*; the organisation is the *account* behind it, not the unit. Recorded in `02` (`assemblies/deal.md`, `told.md` deal-seed) and `00`. *(Generalises the earlier "coalition, not a lone decision-maker" call: the coalition was right; it is now keyed on the deal, not the org domain, so it can cross orgs.)*
- **Goal becomes strategy** — Brain synthesises a human paragraph into an explicit strategy, and needs some things first (a shaped goal, the modules' starting material, a baseline scan). Sketched in `00`, worked in `docs/scenario-councils.md`.
- **Gaps are first-class** → an answer can fail to reach the ground two ways: a **data gap** (a source isn't connected — e.g. LinkedIn for warm paths) or a **told gap** (you haven't supplied something — e.g. the fit conditions). The tool surfaces *what's missing to answer* — connect this, or tell me that — as honestly as it surfaces an answer. Unifies `01`'s unconnected sources and `00`'s goal-shaping questions. Recorded in `00`, worked in `docs/tracing-back.md`.
- **The salesperson's questions are a lens, not the backbone** → MJ's 26 questions are *handles* for showing how an answer traces down to plain facts (and where it can't), **not** the tool's filing system; a contact's memory stays in `02`. A recommendation can be *read* as "a standing question whose answer just changed in a way that points to a move." This settles the reframe; the 26-question probe now lives in `docs/sales-questions.md`, the tracing in `docs/tracing-back.md`.
- **Three sources for any module's knowledge** → anything a module knows is **told** (you supply it), **fetched** (researched via Connections), or **supplied** (handed over by another module when asked). People runs mostly on *fetched*; the supply modules lean on *told* + *supplied*, with *fetched* to ground and propose. Recorded in `03`, worked in `docs/how-modules-collaborate.md`.
- **Setup-side gaps degrade gracefully** → when you can't yet answer what a module asks at setup (e.g. your fit-shape), it doesn't stall: it **researches** a start, **proposes a draft to confirm**, or **flags** it. Unlike a *sensing* gap (usually only flaggable), a *setup* gap the tool can often **fill itself**. The propose-and-confirm move is People's "infer, don't tag" pointed at your own product. Recorded in `03`, worked in `docs/how-modules-collaborate.md`.
- **Modules collaborate by asking, never by ordering** → cross-module work is either a **supplier chain** (one module owns the job and pulls from suppliers; no decision in the middle) or a **Brain play** (a decision in the middle; only Brain conducts). A spectrum runs from answering alone, to one supplier, to a chain, to a Brain play. Reaffirms "request/provide; Brain conducts" from `00`. Worked in `docs/how-modules-collaborate.md`.
- **Every module with initiative has a private memory — same three floors, in tiers** → People remembers relationships; **Profile remembers your voice** (your sent messages + edits, read as facts → patterns → voice — "Profile senses you the way People senses them"); Offerings remembers the playbook and what's landing; Organisation holds the proof library; Connections remembers nothing. Profile + People are **personal** (one per individual), Organisation is **shared**; a module reaches another's memory only by asking. Makes the distributed learning loop concrete. Recorded in `05`/`03`/`04`, worked in `docs/how-modules-collaborate.md`.
- **User input is the *told* layer — identity lives in `05`, supplied outward** → what only the user can supply (no fetched mail reveals it). The foundational case, **who you are** (name, role, "About you"), is owned once in `05` and *lent* to every module that writes or reasons about you, rather than each keeping a copy. Defined on the `02` pilot and in `05`.

### Resolved — deal-primitive session (2026-06-21)
- **The deal is the unit of progress** → you sell into a *deal*, not a person or an organisation, and a deal can **span organisations** (a buyer plus an outside partner, reseller or introducer). **People gathers the deal** (new `02/assemblies/deal.md`, `about: a deal`: the offering, the conversations, and whoever is on them; the *conversation is the membrane*, so membership crosses org lines for free; seeded by a told `deal-seed`, grown by following the threads, later seedable from a Salesforce opportunity). **The Brain stages it** (a scoreboard row is a deal on the goal's ladder; a calendar item is a person inside a deal). The organisation stays the *account*/roster (`stakeholder-map` unchanged). Settles the split cleanly: capability gathers the deal (assistant), intent stages it (Brain). Recorded in `02`, `00`, and root `CLAUDE.md`.
- **Deals are created two ways, one object** → manual (a told `deal-seed`) or **detected** (People clusters conversations and *proposes* a `deal-seed`; you confirm, split or reject; it never auto-commits). Detection is **triggered by a new `buying-intent` read** (price/timeline/proposal/procurement cues on a conversation). Separation: the **offering** is the primary separator, then people-cluster/account, then time; the same-org/same-offering boundary is a **judgement People asks about** rather than guessing. Recorded in `02` (`assemblies/deal.md`, `signals/buying-intent.md`).

### Resolved — catalog-review `04`/`05` session (2026-06-21)
- **The action test — the rule for "is this a signal"** → a **signal** is a read whose change points to a *move on a prospect* (which is why every signal answers one of the 26 questions); a **configuration** is a read or setting that changes how the tool works *with you*. This settles why `04` and `05` are signal-light: they configure, supply and gate, they don't sense prospects. Recorded in `04`/`05` Reasoning; the Brain inherits it as the reusable test.
- **`05` operator dials are configuration, not signals** → fluency, cadence, style, autonomy, coaching tune the tool↔you relationship (and the Brain's pacing), not a prospect move, so by the action test they earn **no signal floor**. They are learned from behaviour by a **learning loop tuning a told seed** (the deferred "second learning loop" below), a different mechanism from a sensing read. `voice` stays `05`'s one signal (operator data *applied to the prospect*, Q23/Q26). Cadence was the closest call and still failed the test. Recorded in `05`.
- **`04` stays signal-less, confirmed** → it supplies and gates; the one judgement-shaped read (proof *strength*) lives in `03`'s `answer-gap`. Recorded in `04`.
- **House voice split from the compliance fence** → the brand voice is now its own `04` assembly `house-voice-guide` (the positive style the render writes *toward*), kept apart from `compliance-fence` (the hard walls it writes *inside*): **emulate vs gate**. The *structure* of the render seam is settled; the **blend math** (how persona-dependently your voice bends toward the house voice) is still open. Recorded in `04`/`05`.
- **Pricing authority gathered into a `pricing-fence`** → `04`'s org-level discount envelope (ceiling, sign-off, floor) now has its own assembly, the envelope the Brain's deferred deal-discount-room reads. Closed an orphaned told record; distinct from `03`'s per-offering price (`price-barrier`). Recorded in `04`.
- **Proof-attributes lineage closed** → `03`'s `answer-gap` reads proof strength off attributes `04` now carries: two told fields on `proof-tag` (named-vs-anonymous, third-party-vs-self) surfaced (with recency from file dates, and proofs-per-claim) by `proof-library`. Recorded in `04`.

## Still open — the *how* (deferred to a use-case pass)
- `02`: the reading's *thresholds* — the **approach** is now set (measure each gap against the relationship's *own rhythm* + an innocent-explanation scan, carry confidence; in `02`'s Reasoning), but the live **numbers** are still open: how many multiples of the normal gap is "gone quiet," how strong a reply clears the bar, how confident a disposition read must be before it's acted on. A use-case pass will tune them. (The decision-maker question is resolved into the stakeholder-map above.)
- `00` **the keystone — triage / pick-and-schedule**: *how* the Brain ranks one move over a hundred quiet ones and lays it on the right day. Now **framed** (it reads the **scoreboard**, weighs the Layer-2 criteria, writes to the **calendar**) but the **mechanism** is still deferred. Plus: the precise representation of a goal, and splitting attention across **several goals** at once.
- **The briefing mechanism** (`00` → modules): the Brain breaks the goal into its own sub-aims and hands each expert a **brief (a job, not a goal)**; the expert turns it into a routine. Shape is recommended (handshake; living briefs; decomposed by Plant/Grow/Nurture) but **not yet written into `00` / `docs/flow.md`**.
- **Reading the fuzzy target**: turning a goal's *mindset* ("feels the legacy-tech pain") into a watchable signal — a Brain↔People↔Offerings bridge. The hardest brief to write.
- **Two reads deferred from `03` Offerings to the Brain** (both *intent*, not product sensing): **best-fit-offering** — given one lead, which of *all* the offerings fits best and why the others fit worse (a Brain play that calls `fit-match` per offering and ranks, not a new Offerings signal); and **deal-specific discount room** — how far to discount on *this* deal to close it (depends on the goal and how hard you push, so it sits with staging; the *static* told discount room is already read by `price-barrier`). The org-level envelope this decision must sit inside is now gathered as `04`'s `pricing-fence`, ready for the Brain to pull. Both belong with the `00` triage keystone above.
- **Tempo vs cadence**: the goal's patience (`00`) and the user's capacity (`05`) push the same "how twitchy / how hard to push" dial — which wins when they disagree?
- **Learning from the user's behaviour**: a second learning loop beyond outcomes — what you act on / ignore / rewrite could tune Profile's dials. This is now the **confirmed refinement mechanism for the dials** (which are settled as *configuration, not signals* — see the action test above): a learning loop tuning a told seed, not a sensing floor. Still not yet drawn.
- `05`: exactly how voice is captured (samples vs style-spec vs learned from edits).
- Learning: the precise mix of passive observation vs. gently asking the user.
- **The deal, the *how*** (the primitive is settled above; these are deferred):
  - **Deal boundaries** — when one coalition becomes two deals (the same org buying two offerings, or one effort splitting), and how a single deal can serve **more than one goal** at once. Ties into `00`'s "several goals at once".
  - **Clustering & grow thresholds** — how readily a new name on a deal's thread or call is pulled in as a member, and how much overlap merges two threads into one deal (the deal's own version of `02`'s own-rhythm thresholds); plus how confident detection must be before it **proposes** versus stays quiet (auto-propose vs always-ask).
  - **CRM seeding** — once Salesforce connects, a CRM opportunity seeds/confirms a deal (account · contact · stage). A **data gap** today (`salesforce` is `connected: false`).
  - **Signal re-scoping (follow-up)** — re-point the signals at the deal: `missing-people` should read the *deal's* buying group rather than the org roster, and `champion` / `blocker` / `loose-end` gain a deal to belong to. Intertwined with the deferred **Brain session** (scoreboard ↔ deal), so do it there.
