# 05 · Persona (You)

**Role:** The voice-and-judgment layer, applied at *generation* time (not a separate reasoning loop). Who you are, how you write, your role, your specific history with each contact. **This is the moat — and the landmine.**

**Supplies**
- How would *I* phrase this? (tone, rhythm, vocabulary, formality)
- What's my actual relationship and history with [person]?
- What's my credibility / angle for this outreach?

**Guardrails**
- An output that's 80% you is worse than 0% you. Bias to **draft, not auto-send** — especially for relationship (sustain) messages.
- Autonomy level should vary by user: cold / volume seller tolerates automation; relationship seller wants approval on every message.

**Open questions**
- How is voice captured — samples, a written style spec, learned from sent messages?
- Personal vs org voice precedence (cross-ref `04`).
