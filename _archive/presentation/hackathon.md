# Design Hackathon — intentions

## What it is
A monthly working session to sketch, build, and explore future ideas for the tool
(we currently call it Trustsell). Each session picks one part of the tool to focus on.

## This session
Topic: **People & Relationships** (the part of the tool about the people you sell to).

The question we want to explore:

> How might we visualise or represent someone's world so they can nurture, grow, and plant
> their relationships?

(nurture = keep relationships alive · grow = move them forward · plant = find new ones)

## What's needed
An intro **presentation** to open the session: it sets up the topic and the question, then leads
into the hands-on part where people sketch and build their own ideas and share them.

## Note
It's a hands-on, creative session. The point is people's own ideas, so this is intentionally just a
statement of intent. The presentation itself is still to be designed.
