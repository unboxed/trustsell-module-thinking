# 02 · Relationships

**Role:** Reason to *answer*. A people **graph** + conversation memory, fed by `01-integrations`. Not a filing cabinet — it does graph traversal (who-knows-whom, warm paths) and semantic retrieval (conversations about a product). It has **no standing goal**; the spine hands it scoped objectives.

**Answers questions like**
- Who's overdue against their *own* cadence? Any open loops I promised to follow up on?
- What stage is this deal; what was the agreed next step; who's champion vs blocker?
- Who's a warm path to [target]? Which happy customers are referral-ready? Which roles in a landed account are still untouched?
- What did we discuss about [product] with [person]?

**Boundary watch:** overlaps with `04-organization` (company context) and `03-offerings` (product mentions). Decide what's "relationship state" vs "org / product fact."

**Open questions**
- Graph schema: what nodes / edges? (people, companies, deals, conversations, products...)
- How is "decay / expected cadence" modeled per contact?
