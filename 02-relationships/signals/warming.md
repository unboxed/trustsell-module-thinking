# Warming

The mirror of cooling, read upward. One contact whose own rhythm is *quickening*: they answer sooner
than they used to, they are the one starting threads now, and more of their colleagues are turning up
on the chain. A single signal carrying its evidence all the way down to a date in the inbox.

| Floor | What People has |
|---|---|
| **Facts** (1) | the dated emails with Priya; who started each thread (the first message's From); the calendar invites she sent; the new names on the cc line this month |
| **Counts** (2) | *normal:* ~3-day median reply, she starts ~1 of every 6 threads, short replies · *now:* ~6-hour median reply (well under her own norm), **she started 4 of the last 6 threads**, two new people from her side joined the chain, replies running longer · *innocent-explanation scan:* the pace lift is not one busy week, it holds across the period |
| **Opinion** (3) | **"Priya is warming"**: pace, initiation and reach all moving the right way at once · *confidence: high* |

What surfaces is not a bare label but *"Priya's warming: she used to take a few days, now she replies
within hours, she's started the last four threads herself, and two of her colleagues have joined in."*
Every clause walks back to a date in the inbox. That is the track-back.

**Threshold.** There is no fixed "replies within a day = warm" line. The lift is measured against
*this person's* own normal (her reply gap shrinking versus her established rhythm, her share of started
threads rising above her usual share). One faster reply is noise; the read fires only when the move
holds across the period and more than one count points the same way. *One count up* → could be a busy
week; *pace **and** initiation **and** reach all up together* → warming is right.

**Why it's trustworthy.** The arguable part is small. Most of the read is *deferred counting*: the
reply gap against her own rhythm, the count of threads she started, the new names from her side, all
deterministic and re-countable. The one genuine judgement, "this is a real lift, not a fluke", is
guarded by requiring the move to hold across the period and to show in more than one count. The
`confidence` is **graded**: high when pace, initiation and reach all turn together; softer to "maybe
warming" when only one count moves.

**Where it can fail to reach ground.** The "more of their people joining in" count leans on seeing who
is on the chain. If a thread moves to a channel People cannot read (a call, a side conversation off
the connected accounts), the reach count thins and confidence falls with it. That is a **data gap**,
named not faked.
