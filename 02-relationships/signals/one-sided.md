# One-sided

A read on the *balance* of a relationship rather than how warm it is. Some contacts run hot and cold
over time; this asks a flatter question that warming and cooling miss: across the whole history, are
you both putting in, or are you doing all the work? It is the read behind a quiet truth, that a
relationship where you start every thread and chase every reply is not really yours yet, however polite
it looks.

| Floor | What People has |
|---|---|
| **Facts** (1) | the threads with Leo; who sent the first message of each; the length of his replies against yours; your "could you...", his "could you..."; your follow-up nudges |
| **Counts** (2) | *who starts:* you opened 9 of the last 10 threads · *reply size:* your notes average 90 words, his replies about 8 · *you give vs they give:* you have offered three things (a deck, an intro, a call), he has offered nothing back · *times you chase:* you have nudged twice to get each reply |
| **Opinion** (3) | **"This one's one-sided"**: you carry every part of it, he answers but never reaches · *confidence: graded* |

What surfaces is not a bare label but *"You're carrying the Leo relationship: you start nearly every
thread, your notes dwarf his replies, and you've offered three things to his none, so the interest may
be more yours than his."* Every clause walks back to a count over the threads. That is the track-back.

**Threshold.** This is a read of *balance*, not direction, which is what separates it from
[`warming`](warming.md): warming asks "is the pace picking *up*?", this asks "is the work *shared*?". A
relationship can be perfectly steady and still deeply one-sided. The read speaks up when who-starts,
reply size and give-and-take all lean the same way across a sustained stretch, not one busy week, and
the imbalance is read against this pair's own history, so a naturally short-replying contact who still
starts threads and offers things is not mistaken for someone just taking.

**Why it's trustworthy.** Every count is plain arithmetic over the threads: who opened each, the word
sizes, what you offered against what they offered, nudges per reply. The one judgement, whether
one-sided is a *problem* here or just the shape of a busy senior contact who is genuinely on board, is
graded and never asserted alone: it is offered as context for the warmth reads, not as a verdict on the
person.

**Where it can fail to reach ground.** If the other side gives back *somewhere the tool cannot see*,
backing you in meetings you do not sit in, or replying by phone, you will look more one-sided than you
are. That is the same off-channel **data gap** the Champion read carries, narrowed where a call was
captured and named not faked where it was not. Read with that caveat, it is a nudge to check, not a
reason to write someone off.
