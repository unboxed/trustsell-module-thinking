# Time to reconnect

## What it means

A read that turns a quiet stretch into a good moment to write.

Some relationships simply drift: no deal, no fallout, you were close a year ago and then life moved on.
On its own that is just a quiet contact. The tool makes this read when the quiet meets a fresh reason
to get back in touch, something that just changed and gives the note a natural hook. It is the mirror
of [`cooling-champion`](cooling-champion.md): cooling is a live relationship slipping away, this is a
long-quiet one worth pulling back.

There is no fixed "quiet for six months" line. How long quiet is measured against your own past rhythm
with the contact (a monthly pair gone silent for a year, not a calendar of days), and the read only
speaks up when a real past relationship and a fresh reason both hold. Quiet on its own is just a cold
contact; a fresh reason against a name you barely knew is a cold approach, not a reconnection. Both
together make the timing.

It leans on the open web for the fresh reason. Without that look there is no change to catch, so the
read thins to "you've gone quiet with this contact" with no hook. And a reconnection that already
happened somewhere the tool cannot see, you bumped into them at an event, leaves the relationship
looking quiet when it is not.

## In practice

A contact you used to trade notes with monthly has been silent for about 11 months (roughly 11 times
the old rhythm), with 20+ two-way messages and two meetings behind you, so a real past relationship,
not a passing name. The open web shows they just moved into a senior transformation role at a new
organisation. The read: "now's a good moment to write again."

## For the build

Applies the shared reading rules in [`docs/reading-principles.md`](../../docs/reading-principles.md).

- Trigger: a real past two-way relationship has gone quiet for far longer than its own usual rhythm,
  AND the open web turns up a fresh, dated reason to write. One without the other is not a moment.
- Confidence: graded. A job move or a fresh win reads as a strong hook; the read softens when the
  reason is thin or the past relationship was light.
- Needs: Web for the fresh reason; without it the read thins to plain "gone quiet" with no timing hook
  (a data gap, named not faked). A reconnection that happened off-channel also leaves the contact
  looking quieter than they are.
- Counts beneath it: the `measures` in the frontmatter (how long quiet against own rhythm, how close
  you were, the fresh reason to write).
