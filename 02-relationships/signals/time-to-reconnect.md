# Time to reconnect

A read that turns a quiet stretch into a good moment to write. Some relationships simply drift: no
deal, no fallout, you were close a year ago and then life moved on. On its own that is just a quiet
contact. This signal speaks up when the quiet meets a **fresh reason to get back in touch**, something
that just changed and gives the note a natural hook. It is the mirror of Cooling champion: cooling is a
live relationship slipping *away*; this is a long-quiet one worth pulling *back*.

| Floor | What People has |
|---|---|
| **Facts** (1) | the history with Maria, once a real two-way thread, nothing for 11 months; what the open web turns up about her and her organisation |
| **Counts** (2) | *how long quiet:* silent 11 months, where the two of you used to trade notes monthly (about 11× the old rhythm) · *how close you were:* 20+ two-way messages, two meetings, she once started threads herself, a real past relationship, not a name that brushed past · *fresh reason to write:* the open web shows she just moved to Head of Transformation at a new council last month |
| **Opinion** (3) | **"Now's a good moment to write to Maria again"**: a strong relationship gone quiet, with a job move that gives you a real reason to reach out · *confidence: graded* |

What surfaces is not a bare label but *"You and Maria were close and went quiet 11 months ago, and
she's just landed a Head of Transformation role, so there's a natural reason to get back in touch
now."* Every clause walks back to the dated history and a dated fact from the open web. That is the
track-back.

**Threshold.** There is no fixed "quiet for six months = write again" line. How long quiet is measured
against your own past rhythm with her (a monthly pair gone silent for a year, not a calendar of days),
and the read only speaks up when a real past relationship and a fresh reason both hold. Quiet on its
own is not a moment, it is just a cold contact; a fresh reason against a name you barely knew is a cold
approach, not a reconnection. Both together make the timing.

**Why it's trustworthy.** How long quiet and how close you were are plain counting against the
relationship's own history, fully re-checkable. The fresh reason is a dated fact from the open web, not
a guess. The one judgement, whether that change is a genuine hook, is graded: a job move or a fresh win
reads stronger than a vague mention, and the read softens when the reason is thin.

**Where it can fail to reach ground.** Two ways. Without the open-web look there is no fresh reason to
catch, so the read thins to plain "you've gone quiet with Maria" with no timing hook: a **data gap**,
named not faked. And a reconnection that already happened somewhere the tool cannot see (you bumped
into her at an event) leaves the relationship looking quiet when it is not, the same off-channel gap
the warmth reads carry.
