# UI Vision — Visualizing My Thinking

## Goal

I'm designing a multi-agent sales tool, and my thinking about it lives as a set of
written modules. Reading them as linear documents makes it hard to *see* the system —
how the pieces relate, where they overlap, and how the whole thing is taking shape.

I want a UI that turns that thinking into something I can look at and explore: a single
visual surface that reflects the current state of my ideas and grows with them.

## What I want it to feel like

- **An infinite canvas.** Not pages or a list — an open, pannable space I can move
  around freely, zoom in and out of, and lay things out spatially.
- **Each thing is a node.** Every module / idea is its own node on the canvas. I want to
  see them as distinct objects I can place, arrange, and relate to one another.
- **A living map of my thinking.** As the thinking evolves, the canvas should reflect it —
  a place to step back and see the whole, and to dive into any one piece.

## Why it matters

Spatial, node-based visualization is how I actually reason about this system. Seeing it
laid out — rather than scrolling through prose — is what helps me catch structure,
connections, and gaps.

## Out of scope

This document is intentionally just the *intent*. The how — tools, layout, interactions,
visual style — is open. Build the best version of this you can.
